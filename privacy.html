Privacy policy

Command Centre · Last updated 24 October 2026

Who uses this app

Command Centre is a private app used by a single person, its owner. It is not available to the public. The only Google account it accesses is the owner's own account, with the owner's consent.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to find bills, renewals, deliveries, claims and similar items that need the owner's attention.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): to include the owner's upcoming appointments in a daily summary.

The app cannot send, delete, change or create emails or calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to produce reminders and summaries for the owner.
  • It is stored on a private server controlled by the owner, which is not reachable from the public internet. Full email bodies are not kept; only short excerpts and references are stored, and excerpts are deleted after 90 days.
  • To summarise or extract information, short excerpts may be processed by AI services that the owner pays for under terms that do not allow the data to be used to train their models.
  • Card numbers, bank account numbers and government ID numbers are never stored.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than providing this service to its owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Command Centre's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. The owner can delete stored data at any time; server backups are kept for no more than 14 days.
