Command Centre

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Command Centre is a personal project used only by its owner. It helps its owner keep track of bills, renewals, deliveries and appointments by reading the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access, and sending the owner a short daily summary.

It is not a public service. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.
